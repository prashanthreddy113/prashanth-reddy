# Mana Bandi — Terms & Conditions (draft v1.0)

> **Draft for legal review.** These are plain‑language terms written so that a person with little reading can understand them when the app reads them aloud. Before launch they must be reviewed by an advocate for the Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines 2020, the Telangana Motor Vehicles Rules, the Consumer Protection (E‑Commerce) Rules 2020, the IT Act 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. The short version is what the app shows; the full version lives on `manabandi.in/terms` and in the owner portal (Settings → Terms), where every new version gets a number and a date. Acceptance (user, version, time, app version) is recorded in the `consents` table.

## D. How acceptance works in the product

- Rider app: **Terms screen** after OTP on first login and whenever `terms_version` changes; the 🔊 button reads the short version aloud; one big "✅ ఒప్పుకుంటున్నాను" button; "📄 పూర్తి నియమాలు" opens the web page.
- Captain app: same screen after OTP, before KYC, with the captain version; the DigiLocker consent is a separate explicit tap inside KYC.
- Owner portal → Settings → Terms: edit Telugu/English text, publish a new version; the apps fetch `/terms/current?audience=rider|captain&lang=te`.
- Backend: `consents(user_id, kind, version, at, ip, app_version)`.
