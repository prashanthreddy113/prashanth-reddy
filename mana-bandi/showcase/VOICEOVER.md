# Mana Bandi — voiceover script (for a professional voice artist)

The videos carry large Telugu + English captions and work without sound (most people watch WhatsApp videos muted). For events, cinema/LED screens and YouTube, record this voiceover and lay it under the video.

**How to record:** one warm, friendly Telangana Telugu voice (not news‑reader Telugu), slow and clear, as if explaining to an elder at the bus stand. Record each line as a separate take. The exact start time of every line is in the matching `.srt` file next to each video (`out/mana-bandi-<role>-16x9.srt`); each line below is one subtitle cue, in the same order. Keep every line inside its cue; if a take is long, trim words rather than speeding up. Add soft background music (royalty‑free, instrumental, folk‑flavoured) at low volume. An English version can be recorded from the right column for YouTube and investors.

## 4. Launch film

The launch film is the three videos joined (customer → captain → owner, about 3½ minutes). For the stage, add a 10‑second opening line spoken live by the host rather than recorded, for example:
"నారాయణఖేడ్, జహీరాబాద్ లాంటి మన పట్టణాల కోసం, మన భాషలో, మన బండి."
("For towns like Narayanakhed and Zaheerabad, in our language: Mana Bandi.")
